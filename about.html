🧊 Cold Plunge Timer

About Us

Our Mission

Cold Plunge Timer was created as a simple way to time a session and use a breathing guide. The tool runs in your browser without an account or an app install.

Why We Built This

We wanted something simple: choose your own timer duration, start it, and breathe. No account or tracking is required.

The built-in box breathing guide offers a way to focus on steady breathing during the cold shock response. We believe a tool should help you stay calm, not add more noise.

Our Values

  • Privacy first. Everything runs in your browser. Your session history never leaves your device.
  • Safety focused. The timer makes no claim about a safe exposure duration.
  • Simplicity. A timer should be a timer. No feature bloat, no distractions.
  • Free to use. The current tool requires no subscription.

About This Site

This is a browser-based tool. Timing, breathing animation, and session history work on your device.
