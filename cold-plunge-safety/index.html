Cold water safety basics

Before you enter

  • Do not enter cold water alone. Have someone nearby who can help.
  • Choose a place with a clear, easy exit and avoid hazards.
  • If you have heart disease, high blood pressure, are pregnant, or have other health concerns, speak with a doctor first.

While in the water

  • Cold water can cause sudden gasping and rapid breathing. Focus on steady, comfortable breathing; keep your head above water.
  • Leave the water immediately if you have chest pain, dizziness, breathing difficulty, confusion, or any other sign that you feel unwell.
  • Do not stay in the water to finish a timer. A timer cannot tell you what is safe for your body.

Read the medical disclaimer. For further guidance, see the Royal Life Saving Society UK and the American Heart Association.
